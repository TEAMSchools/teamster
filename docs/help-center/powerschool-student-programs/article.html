This article shows you how to enroll students in a full-year Student Program in PowerSchool, one at a time or in bulk, and how to exit or remove a record afterward. These designations feed demographics, dashboards, and reporting across the network, so the dates you enter matter.
Who this is for
Staff with PowerSchool Admin access who maintain program rosters: school operations, special education, athletics, and academic program leads.
Before you start
Sign in to PowerSchool Admin for your region through Okta. For bulk enrollment, have the list of student numbers ready.
Looking for something else?
Home instruction and attendance exceptions are also entered as student programs, but they follow their own policies and are not full-year designations. See NJ Home Instruction Request Process or Managing Attendance Exceptions in PowerSchool.

Programs covered and the date rule

These six programs are full-year designations. Pick the name exactly as it appears in the Program dropdown.
Program Regions
Self-Contained Special EducationAll regions
Out of DistrictAll regions
Counseling ServicesNewark only
Student AthleteAll regions
High School Honors ProgramAll regions
High School Tech AcademyAll regions
Critical: use the full academic year
Entry Date is always 07/01 of the year the school year starts. Exit Date is always 06/30 of the year it ends. For the 2026-27 school year that is 07/01/2026 to 06/30/2027. A record with any other dates is not counted as a full-year designation in our systems, even if the student was in the program all year.
Note
Leave Comment, Exit Reason, and Exit Code blank for these programs. Downstream dashboards and tools pick up new or changed records by the end of the next day.

Add one student

  1. Search for the student and open their record.
  2. In the left navigation, click Courses and Programs.
  3. Under Registration, click Student Programs.
  4. Click New. The New Student Program Enrollment form opens.
  5. Leave Comment blank.
  6. In Entry Date, enter 07/01 of the year the school year starts.
  7. In Exit Date, enter 06/30 of the year the school year ends.
  8. Leave Exit Reason blank.
  9. In Program, select the program from the dropdown.
  10. Click Submit. The program appears in the student's Student Programs list with the dates you entered.

Exit a student who left mid-year

When a student leaves a program before the year ends, keep the record and change its exit date. The student stops counting toward the designation from that date, and the time they were enrolled stays on file.
  1. Open the student's record and go to Courses and Programs, then Student Programs.
  2. Click the blue Entry Date link on the program row. The Edit Student Program Enrollment form opens.
  3. Change Exit Date to the date the student left the program.
  4. Leave Exit Reason blank.
  5. Click Submit. The row now shows the new exit date.
The edit form with a mid-year exit date. The same form has the Delete button used in the next section.

Delete a record entered in error

Delete only when the student was never in the program. If they were enrolled for any part of the year, exit them instead so the history is kept.
  1. Open the student's record and go to Courses and Programs, then Student Programs.
  2. Click the blue Entry Date link on the program row.
  3. Click Delete.
  4. Click Confirm Delete. The row disappears from the Student Programs list.

Mass assign a list of students

Use this when you have a list of student numbers, for example from a roster spreadsheet. You build a student selection on the Start Page, then assign the program to everyone in it.
Warning
There is no mass undo. Records created in bulk have to be exited or deleted one student at a time. Check the list before you submit.
  1. On the Start Page, confirm the school and year in the top right are the ones you want.
  2. Click MultiSelect in the row of links under the search bar.
  3. Set Search Field to Student Number.
  4. Paste the student numbers into the box, one per line.
  5. Leave Include Inactive Students unchecked so students who have left the school are not enrolled.
  6. Click Search. The Start Page shows Selection changed to (N) and lists the students found. Confirm N matches your list.
  7. At the bottom right of the Current Student Selection table, click the arrow next to Quick Student Export.
  8. Under Courses and Programs, click Mass Assign Student Programs.
  9. In Program, select the program.
  10. Enter the same full-year Entry Date and Exit Date as for a single student.
  11. Leave Exit Code and Comment blank.
  12. Click Submit. Every student in the selection gets the record. Spot-check one student's Student Programs page to confirm.
