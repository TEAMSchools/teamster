# Candidate dbt tests: NJ SLEDS Staff Course Roster

Drafted 2026-10-20 for the data team planning session. Everything below is a
**proposal** unless marked **Fact**. Rules and page citations come from
`staff-course-roster-fields.md` (keys SCR, StCR, SM, FAQ-CR, FAQ-SM, PS-SCR,
PS-STAFF, PS-DIST, PS-SCHOOL). The test pattern follows issue #5558, "What backs
up a ticketed test".

**Fact** rows labeled "AY2025" are aggregate counts from prod BigQuery, queried
2026-10-20. They cover SY2025-26 sections in Newark, Camden, and Paterson. No
staff-level values appear in this file.

## How to read this

**Eligible assignment (`E`).** A `sectionteacher` row with `allocation = 100`
and a `role` on the teacher-of-record allowlist (lookup B1; today `Lead Teacher`
and `Co-teacher`), on a section in the submission year, not excluded by a
course, section, or staff flag. PowerSchool extracts exactly these rows: "100%
Allocated to a section, in a Role that is not excluded" (PS-SCR).

**Hosts.** `pkg` means the `powerschool` package model, built as a table in each
NJ district, so the failing district project gives the region. `kt` means
kipptaf. kipptaf `int_powerschool__section_teachers` is a view, so a test hosted
there goes dormant. Cross-system kipptaf tests anchor `meta.dagster.ref` to
`int_people__staff_roster` or `int_people__staff_roster_history`, which are
tables.

**#5558 fit.** Step 1 (warn early) needs a host carrying the record ID, school,
region, and period. Package `int_powerschool__section_teachers` has the record
ID (`sectionteacher_id`), the school (`sections_schoolid`), and the region (from
the district project), but no period. Prereq P6 adds the period.

### Key facts behind the rows (AY2025)

- `sectionteacher.allocation` takes only the values 0 and 100.
- Roles in use: `Lead Teacher`, `Co-teacher`, `Gradebook Access (edit)`,
  `Blended Learning`. Paterson uses only the first two.
- Non-teaching roles at 100% (Newark and Camden): `Gradebook Access (edit)` 813
  rows, `Blended Learning` 46 rows. `Co-teacher` at 0%: 10 rows. `Lead Teacher`
  is always 100%.
- Every section has a 100% Lead or Co-teacher row. No section has enrolled
  students but no eligible staff. 238 sections have eligible staff but no
  non-dropped students (Newark 151, Camden 81, Paterson 6).
- 1,251 sections had 2 or more eligible staff during the year, and 231 had 4 or
  more. The most eligible staff on one section: fewer than 10 in Newark and
  Camden, 10 in Paterson. Counting every role and allocation, the most staff on
  one section was 17 (Newark).
- 341 sections had 2 or more Lead Teachers, all one after another with no
  overlap (mid-year changes).
- Eligible `sectionteacher` dates: 0 null, 0 start after end, 0 outside the
  section's term.
- Of 789 eligible staff, by region:
  - 20 have no DOB in PowerSchool.
  - First name: fewer than 10 have a character outside letters, apostrophe, and
    hyphen (fewer than 10 of them a space). Last name: fewer than 10 do.
  - The PowerSchool name differs from the ADP legal name for 60 first names and
    31 last names. Cause: `rpt_powerschool__autocomm_teachers` writes ADP
    `given_name` and `family_name_1` into PowerSchool.
  - 19 match no ADP worker.
- ADP cross-checks, as of each assignment's start date:
  - 22 staff (199 assignments) taught at a school other than their ADP home
    school.
  - 20 staff (116 assignments) have an assignment that ends after their ADP
    termination date.
  - About 30 staff held a paraprofessional or aide title.
  - For most Paterson assignments, the as-of ADP join found no row.

## E. Open questions

1. **Where does "defend downstream" live?** PowerSchool, not dbt, writes the
   state file, so #5558 step 2 has no model for the file-only tests (01-06, 08,
   10-11, 13-18, 21-24). Options: build a kt `rpt_` replica of the staff file as
   the defending model and pre-upload validator (C5), or record a file-only
   exception to #5558.
2. **What builds KTAF's Staff Management file, and does it use ADP legal
   names?** The answer decides whether STCR-07 predicts the 5-field match error.
   Today PowerSchool carries ADP preferred names: 60 first names and 31 last
   names differ from ADP legal names.
3. **What does `NJ_Teacher_Role_Mapping` exclude today, per region?** If
   Gradebook Access (edit) and Blended Learning are not excluded, 859 AY2025
   rows would have been reported as teachers.
4. **Ingestion order.** `s_nj_usr_x` first unblocks 4 rows and firms up 4. Next:
   `s_nj_sec_x` (7 partial rows), then `nj_section_teacher_tracking`.
5. **Policy thresholds.** The co-teacher cap N, and whether it counts every role
   or eligible rows only (P01). Grace days after termination (P03). Whether
   paras and temp staff may ever be teacher of record (P04, P05).
6. **Staff-only sections (STCR-14).** The state is silent, and the PowerSchool
   student extract selects only grade 6+ courses with grades, so K-5 sections
   may legitimately have staff and no students. Keep as a warn worklist, or
   drop?
7. **Do "100% allocated" (PowerSchool), "100% responsible for the grades"
   (PS-SCR purpose), and "100% of the roster" (state) mean the same thing at
   KTAF?** The answer decides STCR-18 and how inclusion teachers are entered.
8. **Leave and substitutes.** The threshold wording differs: "twenty or more
   consecutive days" vs "more than 20 days". And how does ADP mark a long-term
   substitute (title, worker type)? Needed for STCR-19 and 20.
9. **The "current School Year" window** for STCR-10: the section's term, or July
   1 to June 30.
10. **Severity convention.** The repo says staging tests must be
    `severity: error`; #5558 says warn on the earliest routable model. The
    precedent is `stg_powerschool__cc` (warn as an Ops worklist). Most hosts
    here are package int models, so this matters only for C3.
11. **Paterson.** Does Paterson submit Course Roster this cycle? What are its
    CDS codes (B2)? And why does the as-of ADP history join miss most Paterson
    assignments (P02, P03, P05)?
