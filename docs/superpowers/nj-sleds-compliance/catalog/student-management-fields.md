Severity tags used in the element table:

- **[E]** the handbook says "An error will occur" (or equivalent "cannot be
  blank", "must ... or an error will occur").
- **[VC]** a Validation Checks bullet that states a rule without the word
  "error" ("must", "should", "can").
- **[CE]** a Common Errors message the system emits; quoted from the Common
  Errors section.
- **[W]** a Warning. HB p.39: "Warnings are not considered validation errors."
- **[AN]** a requirement stated only in Additional Notes.

## 1. Submission facts

### 1.1 Handbook identity

- Title: "2026-2027 Student Management Data Handbook", "October 2026", "Version
  2.2" (HB p.1).
- Student Management was formerly NJ SMART SID Management: the NJSLEDS Pilot
  v1.0 revision "Updated terminology from SID Management to Student Management"
  (HB p.9).
- v2.2 (October 2026) changes: preschool referral is now
  SpecialEducationClassification = 19 AND GradeLevel in 3H, 3F, 4H, 4F, 5H, 5F;
  GradeLevel must be a preschool grade for code-19 preschool referrals;
  IEPGraduationCourseRequirement, IEPGraduationAttendance and
  FAFSACompletionIndicator are "required only between the dates of April 1-June
  30 of the current school year" (HB p.6-7).
- v2.0 (August 2026): DistrictEntryDate and SchoolEntryDate accept future dates
  "up to the applicable snapshot date" (HB p.7).

Handbook date windows that validation rules depend on:

- Future entry dates: "For the Fall Student Management Submission, future
  DistrictEntryDates from July 1 to October 15 are accepted. For the End of Year
  Student Management Submission, future DistrictEntryDates from October 16-June
  30 are accepted." The SchoolEntryDate wording is the same (HB p.67, p.76).
- Annual attendance reporting period: "July 1 through June 30" (HB p.133, 135,
  173, 175, 177, 179).
- April 1-June 30 of the current school year: IEPGraduationCourseRequirement,
  IEPGraduationAttendance, FAFSACompletionIndicator (HB p.142, 144, 183).
- "after the Fall Snapshot for the End of Year Snapshot": NumberOfDaysPresent,
  NumberOfDaysAbsent, RemoteDaysPresent, RemoteDaysAbsent,
  FederalHSMathTestingReq, DeafHardOfHearing (HB p.133, 135, 140, 175, 177,
  181).
- "after the Fall Snapshot through the High School Graduation Snapshot":
  Biliterate (HB p.123).
- "After the End of Year Snapshot and through the Graduation Snapshot": ELA/Math
  pathway must be O or P for active 12th graders (HB p.114, 117).
- Class of 2027 graduation assessment rules "apply to any student graduating
  between September 1, 2026 and August 31, 2027, regardless of grade level" (HB
  p.114, 117).

### 1.3 Students in scope

General:

- "Districts are responsible for submitting records for every student under
  their care" (WB p.4).
- "Districts must report enrollment data for every student they are responsible
  for. Multiple records should be submitted when a student's enrollment changes,
  but your LEA remains the reporting authority" (WB p.6). Examples: in-district
  school transfer, transfer to an APSSD mid-year, out-of-district placement
  still reported by the LEA.
- Which LEA submits is set by the Reporting Responsibilities scenarios (RR). For
  a charter school, Scenario 10: the student's resident district/school is
  District 1 (School A) and the student attends a charter or Renaissance school
  in District 2 (School B); the charter is the Submitting District, and the
  charter is Receiving and Attending (RR p.7). Scenario 21: a charter student
  who attends another district's program is still submitted by the charter (RR
  p.11).
- The CDS workbook's Intro adds: "Charter Schools, Private Schools, and other
  similar locations are not considered resident locations."

Fall Snapshot file contents (WB p.36):

- "Active records for students who: Currently are attending a school within your
  district or who you are currently responsible for reporting to Student
  Management; Transferred into a school within your district or into your
  reporting responsibility after the 25-26 school year End-of-Year Student
  Management Snapshot."
- "Inactive records for students who: Transferred out after the last day of the
  25-26 school year; Attended your district in 25-26 but withdrew before June
  30th."

First file of the new school year (WB p.16): inactive records for students who
transferred out after the prior school year; inactive records for prior-year
graduates ("Even if these students have already been marked as Inactive, they
must still appear in the file to ensure proper validation"); active records for
all returning students; active records for all new students.

Keeping inactive records (WB p.17; HB p.133, 175):

- "Students active at any point during the current school year must remain in
  Student Management through the Fall and End-of-Year Snapshots. Their inactive
  records are needed for validating other submissions, such as the Course
  Roster."
- "Students expected to return but who withdraw over the summer should remain as
  Inactive in Student Management until after the Fall Snapshot is completed."
- "Students who attended at least one day must remain in Student Management
  through the End-of-Year Snapshot."
- HB: "All active and inactive records of the reporting year must remain in
  Student Management so that they are captured in the End of Year Snapshot. This
  includes inactive records reported to the Fall Snapshot."

Special populations:

- Graduates: "Current-year graduates should remain active through the End of
  Year Student Snapshot. Districts typically report graduates as inactive
  between July 6 and August 31, but the exit date should represent the student's
  graduation date (often in June)" (HB p.81, Warning).
- Never attended: SchoolExitDate = first day of school, NumberOfDaysPresent = 0,
  NumberOfDaysAbsent = 0 (WB p.13).
- Register change within the same school: "Update the existing record, Do not
  create a new enrollment record" (WB p.13).
- Preschool: "All preschool-age students must be reported in Student Management
  to obtain a SID", including state-mandated, tuition-paying, and
  special-education referred or receiving (WB p.12).
- Non-Public: report only if enrolled in a private school and receiving special
  education services; reported by the public district where the private school
  is located; exit with PPE (WB p.12). Non-Public students "are reported only in
  the Student Submission" and "must not be reported in the Special Education
  Submission" (HB p.109).
- Shared-time vocational: both the non-vocational LEA and the vocational school
  report the student (WB p.11; RR p.6).

PowerSchool selection (PS-R, Selection Criteria and Report Input):

- Enrolled at some time between the report start and end dates;
  `[S_NJ_STU_X]IncludeInNJSMART_TF` = Yes; the "Exclude this enrollment from NJ
  SLEDS SID" flag not set.
- "An additional record is extracted if the District Entry Date for an
  enrollment has changed during the reporting period."
- The extract "creates a record for each different value in the School Code
  Attending field from the previous enrollment during the reporting period"
  (PS-R #29).
- The Report End Date sets grade level, district status, and student status.
  "NJSLEDS does not accept uploads for prior years."
- Inputs include Fall Snapshot (Yes/No), Legal vs Preferred name, include
  unassigned SIDs, "Date Report Last Submitted to the State" (drives
  SpecialEducationClassification), include graduated students, include no-show
  students.

### 1.4 File format

- Upload format is ".CSV UTF-8" (WB p.19, 24, 31, 33; SID p.1). File name
  maximum is 80 characters (WB p.24).
- Templates: "Template A contains headers and lookup values, and Template B
  contains headers only. Must be exported as a .CSV UTF-8 file for upload" (WB
  p.19).
- Both templates have the same 104 header columns, with no filler column. TMPL-A
  adds a `Lists` sheet (28 lookup lists) and in-cell data validations.
- Header spelling differs from the handbook in one place: TMPL header
  `EligibleforLIEP` (lower-case f); the handbook uses `EligibleForLIEP` (HB
  p.95).
- Repeating elements: WorldLanguageAssessment and WorldLanguagesAssessed "must
  be repeated 5 times in the file ... even if the additional four iterations are
  not needed" (HB p.125). HomeLanguage is also repeated 5 times (HB p.154).
- Dates are YYYYMMDD with no separators (e.g. HB p.48, 97). YearOfGraduation is
  YYYY (HB p.75).
- Blank, not "null": v1.8 "Updated guidance to clarify that fields may be left
  blank, rather than being reported as 'null'" (HB p.7).
- Leading zeros: LID must keep them, stored as alphanumeric (HB p.40). The CDS
  fields became type Character in August 2024 (HB p.11).
- NJSLEDS cannot be edited directly; the SIS is the source system (WB p.28).
  "The system will override any records where errors have been resolved" on
  re-upload (WB p.33).
- Record statuses named in guidance: Error, Sync, Unresolved, Transfer (WB p.7);
  Associated SID, New SID, Unresolved (SID p.1).

## 2. Element table

There are 92 elements in handbook order (HB TOC p.2-5). With the 5-way repeats
that makes 104 file columns.

"Mandatory" means the handbook says the element is mandatory for all students.
The listed population exemptions (Non-Public, preschool referral, shared-time
vocational students reported by the vocational school) are given verbatim.
"Conditional" means the requirement depends on another value, a grade, a status,
or a date window.

ReportedSharedVoc = Y ("shared time vocational school students being reported by
the vocational school"). **PSR** = preschool referral
(SpecialEducationClassification 19 AND GradeLevel 3H/3F/4H/4F/5H/5F, HB p.6).

## 3. Cross-record and cross-submission rules

### 3.1 Uniqueness and record grain

- One active full-time record per SID: "An error will occur if two or more
  records with matching SID numbers have a SchoolStatus of A and EnrollmentType
  of F" (HB p.42).
- No duplicate active matches within an LEA: an error if records with the same
  Submitting District, SID, Local ID, First Name, Last Name and Date Of Birth
  have SchoolStatus = A (HB p.170).
- LID is unique within the district: an error if "multiple students within the
  district with the same LID are reported" (HB p.40). The LID must stay the same
  throughout the student's enrollment (HB p.40).
- One record per enrollment: report a new record when enrollment changes (WB
  p.6). Examples: an in-district transfer is two records, School 001 exited with
  T2 and School 002 entered with SPECED (WB p.9). Leaving NJ and returning is
  two records in the same school, the first exited with T8 (WB p.10).
- Register change within the same school: update the existing record (WB p.13).
- PowerSchool grain: one record per distinct School Code Attending across
  enrollments, plus an extra record when District Entry Date changed in the
  period (PS-R Selection Criteria; PS-R #29).

### 3.2 SID existence and identity matching

- SIDs "must be valid 10-digit state identification numbers as issued by
  NJSLEDS" (HB p.42).
- A blank SID on an active upload starts identity matching. Outcomes: Associated
  SID, New SID, or Unresolved. Associated and New records "need another upload
  to continue processing" (SID p.1).
- The key identity fields matched against the ID Master are FirstName,
  MiddleName, LastName, DateOfBirth and Gender (UNR p.1; SID p.2). "When you
  associate a record, the ID Master data overwrites your submitted data in the
  key identity fields" (UNR p.1).
- "Never leave the SID or SMID field blank on a future upload once it has been
  assigned. If the ID field is blank, NJSLEDS will rerun identity matching and
  may create multiple IDs" (UNR p.2). "Creating a second SID for the same
  student can require a SID merge" (SID p.1).
- Corrections to ID Master data go through a Field Change Request case; do not
  re-upload until it is confirmed (UNR p.2).

### 3.3 Status combinations and transfers between LEAs

- DistrictStatus = I with SchoolStatus = A is an error (HB p.62, 170).
- SID blank with DistrictStatus = I and/or SchoolStatus = I is an error (HB
  p.42).
- SchoolStatus = I requires SchoolExitDate, SchoolExitWithdrawalCode,
  NumberOfDaysPresent, NumberOfDaysAbsent, NumberOfStateExcusedAbsences,
  RemoteDaysPresent and RemoteDaysAbsent (HB p.170).
- Transfer Requests between LEAs (WB p.8): the Associated (prior) district
  accepts by inactivating and re-uploading "with DistrictStatus and SchoolStatus
  = 'I'". The Requesting district waits, then re-uploads as Active.

### 3.4 Date logic

- SchoolEntryDate cannot be before DistrictEntryDate (HB p.76, CE).
- Entry dates may be in the future only within the current snapshot window (Fall
  July 1-October 15; EOY October 16-June 30) (HB p.67, 76).
- SchoolEntryDate does not reset each school year while the student stays Active
  in the school (HB p.76). DistrictEntryDate is the most recent district entry
  (HB p.67).
- SchoolExitDate and SchoolExitWithdrawalCode must both be present or both be
  blank (HB p.78-79). Both are blank for SchoolStatus = A (HB p.78, 81).
- The exit date is the day after the last day attended. A no-show's exit date is
  the first day of school (WB p.13).
- LIEP chain: LIEPStartDate < LIEPEndDate < LIEPStartDate2 < LIEPEndDate2.
  LIEPStartDate and LIEPEndDate cannot be in the future (HB p.97, 99, 101, 103).
- DateOfBirth gives an age of 2-105 overall and 2-22 for GradeLevel PK-12 (HB
  p.48, 87).

### 3.5 Cross-snapshot rules

- MigrantStatus, ImmigrantStatus and Homeless raise a warning between the Fall
  and EOY Snapshots if the value differs from the Fall Snapshot (HB p.150, 157,
  162).
- SpecialEducationClassification code 00 (HB p.92):
  - Fall: report 00 if an initial evaluation found the student ineligible
    "during the prior reporting period (e.g., on or after July 1)"; do not
    report 00 in later Fall submissions without a new determination.
  - EOY: an ineligibility determined after October 15 may be reported as 00, and
    the determination must still be reported in the next Fall submission.
- Code 99: "underwent a reevaluation as of the last snapshot date" that found no
  continued disability (HB p.93). Code 19: an initial evaluation is pending as
  of the snapshot date (HB p.93). The Required section's category 2 is "on or
  after July 1 of the prior reporting year (e.g., July 1, 2025)" (HB p.91). PS
  uses the "Date Report Last Submitted to the State" input for this (PS-R Report
  Input).
- ImmigrantStatus must be Y if FirstEntryDateIntoAUSSchool is "less than 3 years
  before the Snapshot date" (HB p.158). "All periods of attendance must be
  counted toward the 'three full academic years'" (HB p.157).
- LIEP four-year warnings: measured from July 1 of the current school year,
  current-year records only (HB p.98, 102).
- LIEPParentRefusalDate before July 1 of the current school year raises a
  warning (HB p.105).
- EighthTechnologicalLiteracy: all grade 08 are NE at the Fall Snapshot; results
  are reported in "the August Student Management collection file" (HB p.151).
- Retained: at EOY, a student to be retained next year is N until the next year
  starts (HB p.90).

### 3.6 Graduation and cohort rules

- Class of 2027: graduation assessment requirements "apply to any student
  graduating between September 1, 2026 and August 31, 2027, regardless of grade
  level" (HB p.114, 117).
- An L exit after September 1, 2023 requires an ELA and Math pathway in D, E, H,
  I, J, K, M, N, Q or S (HB p.114, 117).
- Pathway O, P or R cannot have SchoolExitWithdrawalCode L (HB p.114, 117).
- Active 12th graders after the EOY Snapshot through the Graduation Snapshot
  must have O or P (HB p.114, 117).
- "For students who were eligible to take the NJGPA/NJGPA-A but did not do so
  ... The value of O must be selected" (HB p.114).
- D, E, H, I, J, K, M or N may be used for Class of 2027 students who did not
  take NJGPA-A only if they enrolled after the Spring 2027 administration, met
  all other requirements, and will graduate before the Summer 2027
  administration (HB p.114).
- Pathway M graduates are excluded from the federal ACGR and counted in the
  state rate (HB p.114, 117). IEPGraduation* = 1 removes the student from the
  federal ACGR numerator (HB p.142, 144).
- FAFSA: an L exit after September 1, 2025 requires C or E. Grade 12 with an O
  or P pathway and no L exit requires N (HB p.183).
- Graduates stay active through the EOY Snapshot. They are typically inactivated
  July 6-August 31, with the graduation date as SchoolExitDate (HB p.81).
- The first file of a new year must include prior-year graduates as inactive "to
  ensure proper validation" (WB p.16).
- Biliterate: the "March 1 Biliteracy snapshot" pathway guidance is to report N
  for a planned portfolio appeal; R "may delay the delivery of student seals"
  (HB p.114-115).

### 3.7 Retention of records across the year

- All active and inactive records of the reporting year must stay in Student
  Management through the EOY Snapshot, including inactive records already in the
  Fall Snapshot (HB p.133, 175; WB p.17).
- Inactive records are used to validate other submissions, such as the Course
  Roster (WB p.17).
- Removal: students not returning whose inactive enrollment was already captured
  in a snapshot may be removed at the start of the new year. Summer withdrawals
  stay inactive until after the Fall Snapshot (WB p.17).

### 3.8 Shared-time and multi-LEA reporting

- Shared-time vocational students are reported by both LEAs, each with
  EnrollmentType = S. Attendance is split 0.5 per day at each school. CDS for
  both records: Resident is the home address, Attending is the vocational
  school, Receiving is the non-vocational school (WB p.11).
- ReportedSharedVoc is Y only from the vocational LEA (HB p.180).
- For shared-time students the Attending school cannot equal the Receiving
  school (HB p.63).
- Attendance is reported per enrollment record, for time in that school only (WB
  p.14).
- Chronic absenteeism: Absentee Rate = NumberOfDaysAbsent /
  (NumberOfDaysPresent + NumberOfDaysAbsent). State-excused absences are
  excluded (ATT p.1).
- RemoteDaysPresent cannot exceed NumberOfDaysPresent, and RemoteDaysAbsent
  cannot exceed NumberOfDaysAbsent (HB p.133, 135).

### 3.9 Rules that depend on the CDS reference file

- Every Resident, Receiving and Attending code must be in the CDS list.
  SchoolCodeResident must be a regular public school. Receiving and Attending
  school codes cannot be non-operational schools (HB p.64-74).
- GradeLevel must be offered at the Attending school ("Grades Offered").
  Otherwise ProgramTypeCode must be 18-33 or 38 (HB p.87, 89; WB p.32).
- EnrollmentType S requires a vocational Attending school (HB p.63).
  ReportedSharedVoc = Y requires a vocational submitting district (HB p.180).
- NonPublic REC or NREC warns if the Attending CDS is not a NonPublic location
  (HB p.110).
- TuitionCode 06 requires the submitting district to be a Choice District (HB
  p.83).
- DaysOpen ranges depend on the Attending district's Preschool Expansion Aid
  flag and on districts 3400-3403 (HB p.173).
- WB p.32: "If your district is sending students to a school you used in prior
  years but did not reapply for, an error will occur." "Reporting students under
  the wrong LEA or CDS Codes can result in rejected records."

### 3.10 Reporting-responsibility codes (RR)

- Out-of-state resident school not listed: ZZ-0000-000 (RR p.9, Scenario 17; HB
  p.64-66).
- State-responsible students: Resident 21-8909-100, or 21-8501-024 if DCF pays
  tuition (RR p.9, Scenario 16).
- Out-of-state student in a juvenile detention center: Resident 21-8909-100;
  Submitting and Receiving JJC 21-8503-975; Attending is the JDC (RR p.12,
  Scenario 23).
- Home instruction: use the CDS codes the student would have if not receiving
  home services. Group home: the parent's address sets the Resident codes.
  Homeless: last permanent address for the first 12 months, then the district of
  domicile; disputes go to the County Superintendent (RR p.13).
- Non-Public students are not reported in the Special Education submission (HB
  p.109).

## 5. What the state rejects vs. warns

- The handbook defines the two levels (HB p.39). "Validation Checks: Specific
  data validation that will occur with respect to each data element." "Warnings:
  (if applicable) The warning message indicating that the data entered does not
  meet the standards outline by the NJDOE. Warnings are not considered
  validation errors."
- The webinar adds (WB p.20): "Validation Checks: what will trigger errors";
  "Warnings: will not block submission but still need attention."
- Processing (WB p.25-33):
  - After upload the file shows "In Process", then "Processed".
  - An email reports file status and the "Number of errors and warnings".
  - The Error Report shows "Record Level errors" and an "Error By Type" view.
    Records with errors carry an "Error" status.
  - Fixing means correcting the SIS and re-uploading; "The system will override
    any records where errors have been resolved."
- Errors are record-level. WB p.32: "Reporting students under the wrong LEA or
  CDS Codes can result in rejected records."
- Records in Unresolved, Associated SID or New SID status do not finish
  processing until re-uploaded (SID p.1; UNR p.2).
- Certification: "Submission of data is considered a formal certification that
  the information is accurate" (HB p.38; WB p.37).

## 6. Open questions

Ordered by likely impact on a KTAF charter file. Items 1-5 are the top five.

1. **The PowerSchool extract layout does not match the state template.**
   - PS-R defines 97 output items, including a 4-character "Filler" at item 65.
     Both NJDOE templates have 104 named columns and no filler.
   - Both put Homeless before HomelessPrimaryNighttimeResidence; the handbook
     TOC has them the other way round.
   - PS-R gives date formats as "MM/DD/YYYY" (DateOfBirth, entry/exit, LIEP
     dates); the handbook requires YYYYMMDD.
   - PS-R lengths differ from the handbook: GenerationCodeSuffix 3 vs 4,
     TitleIIndicator 5 vs 2, NumberOfDaysPresent/Absent and RemoteDaysAbsent 3
     vs 5, WorldLanguagesAssessed 3 vs 40.
   - Not stated anywhere: whether the PS file drops the filler and reformats
     dates on output, or whether NJSLEDS maps columns by header name or by
     position.
2. **FAFSACompletionIndicator dates conflict.**
   - The element page says the C/E rule applies to L exits "after September 1,
     2025" (HB p.183); revision v1.5 says "September 1, 2024" (HB p.8).
   - v2.2 says the field is "required only between the dates of April 1-June 30"
     (HB p.7), yet a Validation Check still requires it for active grade 12
     "During the End-of-Year (EOY) submission window", which opens October 19,
     2026 (CAL).
   - "Unless an exemption applies" is defined only in revision history.
3. **The preschool referral definition is circular.**
   - v2.2 defines preschool referral as SpEd 19 AND GradeLevel 3H-5F (HB p.6,
     86).
   - The GradeLevel check still says "An error will occur if the field is left
     blank unless student is preschool referred" (HB p.87).
   - Many other exemptions ("student is preschool referred") therefore depend on
     GradeLevel already being populated.
   - How the state treats an SpEd 19 record with a blank GradeLevel is not
     stated.
4. **PowerSchool code lists and rules are out of date against the handbook.**
   - ELA/Math pathway: PS lists A, B, C, F, G, L, W, and S as "Class of 2023
     Graduates Only". PS-R says "The biliterate field must be a Yes if the ELA
     graduation pathway indicator is not blank" (the reverse of HB p.123). PS-S
     Math note says "cannot be 'N' or 'O'" with L (HB: O, P, R).
   - LanguageInstructionEducationalProgram: PS-R lists value 6 (HB p.122: "no
     longer a valid value").
   - WorldLanguageAssessment: PS-R lists OPIOTHER and lacks AAPPL ASL, ALTA,
     ASLT, NEWL, STAMP LAT, STAMP ASL.
   - MilitaryConnected: PS-S says "Blank - Not connected" with no value 1 (HB
     p.112: report 1; blank is an error).
   - InDistrictPlacement: PS-S says "SPEDCED".
   - Whether the extract code (not just the help pages) still emits these values
     is unknown.
5. **The ELPScreenerDate checks contradict each other** (HB p.107). One bullet
   says it "is required for K-12 students who are reported as eligible"; the
   next says that with EligibleForLIEP Y or N and a non-preschool grade,
   "ELPScreenerDate can be blank". The Common Error then says a K-12 eligible
   student must have a screener date. v2.1 says the "can" wording is a
   correction (HB p.7).
6. **Which TuitionCode applies to charter students.** 07 = "Charter school
   student: tuition is based on resident district of the charter school". The
   only charter-specific requirement is "03" for the first year after coming
   from a non-public school, then "07" (HB p.82). Not stated: whether every
   charter student must carry 07, or whether a blank is accepted ("Leave blank
   if not applicable").
7. **SchoolEntryDate future dates.** The Common Error still says "Date cannot be
   in the future" (HB p.76); v2.0 allows future dates within the snapshot
   window. Also, the handbook says SchoolEntryDate "does not reset" each year,
   while PS-R takes `[Students]EntryDate` / `[Reenrollments]EntryDate` of the
   reporting enrollment unless an override is set. Whether those match for
   continuing students depends on how year-to-year enrollments are stored, which
   PS-R does not address.
8. **"Status" is undefined** in SchoolEntryCode ("Status = A", p.172),
   NumberOfDaysPresent ("If Status = I", p.175) and FederalHSMathTestingReq
   ("status = A", p.140). The old "Status" element was renamed DistrictStatus in
   2021 (HB p.18), but SchoolStatus was added at the same time.
9. **FederalHSMathTestingReq has an unconditional blank check.** The third
   check, "If the NonPublic field is blank, an error will occur if the
   FederalHSMathTestingReq is blank" (HB p.140), has no grade or exit-code
   limit, unlike the Required section.
10. **DaysOpen range and length.** The handbook allows 0-250 for districts
    without Preschool Expansion Aid other than 3400-3403 (HB p.173). PS-R
    ("between 180 and 250") and TMPL-A (whole 180-250) are narrower. Length
    "Minimum 3, Maximum 3" conflicts with values under 100.
11. **State-excused absence reasons do not add up.** HB p.179 says there are
    "seven allowable reasons" but lists six (no Veterans Day). ATT p.2 lists
    seven, including "Participation in observance of Veterans Day (N.J.S.A.
    18A:36-13.2)", and limits the civic event to "one day for students in grades
    6 through 12".
12. **Attendance requirements at Fall.** The handbook makes
    NumberOfStateExcusedAbsences mandatory for all students (p.179), and
    NumberOfDaysAbsent's blank check has no Fall exemption (p.177). The Required
    sections and PS-R say these are not required for active students at the Fall
    Snapshot. PS writes 0 when there is no attendance data.
13. **Half-day values do not fit some lengths.** NumberOfStateExcusedAbsences
    max length 3 cannot hold "100.5". TMPL-A validations for days
    present/absent/excused are whole numbers 0-366 only, which rejects the
    shared-time halves the handbook allows.
14. **ZZ-0000-000 is not in the reference lists.** The handbook and RR Scenario
    17 say to use ZZ/0000/000 for an unlisted out-of-state resident school (HB
    p.64-66; RR p.9). The CDS Codes sheet has no ZZ or 0000 rows, and the TMPL-A
    Counties list has no ZZ (it lists 80 and CT, DE, ID, MA, MD, NC, NH, NY,
    PA).
15. **CountryOfBirth type.** It is Numeric 1-4 but accepts "R" (HB p.52). PS-R
    writes "USA" when Country of Birth is null, which is neither a numeric NCES
    code nor 2330.
16. **WorldLanguagesAssessed: codes or names?** The handbook gives max length 40
    and states rules in names ("Latin", "American Sign Language", "eng/english",
    HB p.128). TMPL-A and PS store 3-letter codes. TMPL-A's assessment list has
    "NEWT" where the handbook has NEWL, and lacks "AAPPL ASL".
17. **GenerationCodeSuffix length.** HB max is 4 since August 2024 (HB p.11,
    46). TMPL-A validation and PS-R allow 3. PS-R also says the suffix "is
    appended to the student's last name".
18. **Biliteracy and graduation snapshot dates are uncertain.** The handbook's
    "March 1 Biliteracy snapshot" (HB p.114) differs from the calendar's "Seal
    of Biliteracy Submission Closes" March 10, 2027, which the page source flags
    "CONFIRM YEAR". No 2026-27 Graduation Snapshot window appears on the
    calendar. EighthTechnologicalLiteracy refers to "the August Student
    Management collection file" (HB p.151), which is not on the calendar.
19. **Biliterate with world-language fields populated.** Revision v1.3 (HB p.8)
    says populated world-language fields mean "Biliterate must = Yes (Y)". The
    element page (HB p.123) only errors when Biliterate = N. Whether blank
    Biliterate with populated world-language fields is an error is not stated.
20. **LIEPStartDate2 eligibility rule.** Its Common Error fails when
    EligibleForLIEP is "blank or N" (HB p.102). The October 2024 amendment
    narrowed the equivalent LIEPStartDate rule to blank only (HB p.10).
21. **Math pathway for Biliterate students.** Its Required section includes "all
    students with a BiliterateValue of Y" (HB p.116), but its Validation Checks
    have no Biliterate clause (HB p.117).
22. **Pathway rule scope.** "regardless of grade level" (HB p.114, 117) sits
    beside revision v1.5's "Limited graduation pathway rule to Grade 12 students
    only" (HB p.8) and the heading "For 12th graders and graduates only".
23. **StudentLearningEnvironment = 1 wording.** It errors when RemoteDaysPresent
    and RemoteDaysAbsent "are both not 0" (HB p.131). The Remote fields' own
    checks error on either one being non-zero (HB p.133, 135).
24. **DistrictStatus on an in-district transfer.** WB p.9 shows the exited
    School 001 record with DistrictStatus A and exit code T2 (its column header
    reads "Leaves NJ", apparently copied from p.10). The handbook does not say
    what DistrictStatus an in-district exited school record should carry.
25. **Fall Snapshot scope wording.** WB p.36 lists "Attended your district in
    25-26 but withdrew before June 30th" among the inactive records the 2026-27
    Fall file must include. WB p.17 says records already captured in a snapshot
    may be removed at the start of the new year.
26. **Gender source in PowerSchool.** PS-R #7 says the report extracts
    `[Students]Gender`, "which may differ from the Legal Gender". Gender is a
    key identity field for ID Master matching (UNR p.1).
27. **PowerSchool field names differ between the report spec and the setup
    page.**
    - DistrictEntryDate: `[Students]` vs `[S_NJ_STU_X]`.
    - LIEP start/end: `LEPBeginDate`/`LEPEndDate` vs
      `LIEPStartDate`/`LIEPEndDate`.
    - Homeless: `[S_NJ_STU_X]Homeless` vs `[Students]homeless_code`.
    - Military: `Military_Connected_Indicator` vs `ActiveMilitaryConnected`.
    - Gifted: `[S_NJ_STU_X_]Gifted_And_Talented` vs `Gifted and Talented`.
    - Biliterate vs Biliterate_YN; LearningEnvironment vs
      StudentLearningEnvironment; ProgramTypeCode vs PTC; EntryCode vs
      school_entry_code; DeafHardOfHearing vs Deaf.
    - FAFSA vs FASFA (both on `[S_STU_X]`).
    - LIEPLanguageOfInstruction from `LIEP_Type` (PS-R) vs a separate
      `LIEP Language` field (PS-S).
    - LanguageInstructionEducationalProgram on table `[S_NJ_STU]`.
    - The Days Absent override on PS-S is `[S_NJ_STU_X]CumulativeStateAbs`, the
      same field as State Excused.
28. **ProgramTypeCode and Retained exemptions.**
    - ProgramTypeCode's Required section and Validation Checks do not exempt
      Non-Public students (HB p.88-89), but PS blanks the field for NP.
    - Retained's check omits the preschool-referral exemption that its Required
      section grants (HB p.90).
    - StudentInternetConnectivity's check omits the preschool-referral exemption
      that its Required section grants (HB p.137).
29. **IEPGraduation SpEd range.** The 01-17 range excludes code 19 (HB p.142,
    144). Whether a grade 12 student with code 19 in April-June is exempt is not
    stated.
30. **The handbook references values that are not file columns.**
    "DistrictCodeSubmitting" / "Submitting District" (HB p.83, 170, 180) appear
    in rules but not in the file. NJSLEDS presumably derives them from the
    uploading LEA; the handbook does not say so.
31. **"Non-operational school codes."** HB p.70, 74 reject them, but the CDS
    file has no school-level non-operational flag. The closest `SchoolCategory`
    value is "Non Operating district".
32. **ImmigrantStatus Common Error typo.** Its resolution says "CountyOfBirth"
    (HB p.158), meaning CountryOfBirth.
