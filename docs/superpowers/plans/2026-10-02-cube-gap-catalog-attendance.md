### Notes

- **Biggest correction to the task brief**: the task's Asana context says
  "attendance covers all four regions unless the workbook shows otherwise" — the
  workbook shows otherwise, clearly and consistently. Every region filter in
  every live dashboard except "Miami - NSLP" is a hard-coded `union` of exactly
  Camden/Newark/Paterson; Miami is excluded, not merely unfiltered. "Miami -
  NSLP" is the inverse: Miami-only. There is no single tab in this workbook
  showing all four regions together. Any Cube-connector replacement needs to
  either (a) keep two surfaces (NJ tri-region + Miami) matching this split, or
  (b) explicitly decide to unify them — a design decision, not a parity gap,
  since Cube's `student_attendance_enrollment_*_view`s already cover all four
  regions uniformly (the RLS/`access_policy` applies at the viewer level, not a
  hard-coded region list).
- Two archived/orphaned dashboards inside the .twb
  (`ARCHIVE__CA Intervention Tracker`, `Successful Calls Tracking - Old`) share
  a `repository-location id` with their live same-domain counterparts, which is
  the Tableau artifact of "duplicate sheet, rename, leave old one behind" — not
  evidence of two live surfaces. Excluded from the measure/dimension inventory
  as dead weight. `Suspension Rate Over Time` / `Suspensions` have no live view
  at all; comm-log sheets reference `is_oss`/`is_iss` still, so the underlying
  data isn't gone, just this pair of dashboards.
- The "Attendance Metric" parameter swap (ADA / %On-Time / Chronic Absenteeism /
  Chronic Tardiness / Truant / HS Alt) is the single biggest structural gap for
  a 1:1 Cube connector: Tableau lets a user flip one parameter to see 6
  different rates on the same viz; Cube has all 6 as separate named measures
  across two views (daily vs periods, split by day-weighted vs student-weighted
  per `.claude/rules/cube-authoring.md`), with no single member that reproduces
  the toggle. A Cube-backed replacement likely needs either 6 separate chart
  variants or a client-side (Tableau/BI-layer) parameter action switching which
  Cube measure is queried.
- "HS Alt" (`is_present_hs_alt`, `present_weight`-adjacent) has no Cube
  equivalent found anywhere in the model — flagging as mart-ready but unverified
  beyond its presence as a raw mart column; did not chase its full definition.
- Chronic-absenteeism/tier thresholds in the workbook (`<=0.9` for chronic,
  `<0.795` for "chronic tardy") are recomputed from scratch in Tableau calc
  formulas rather than reading `ada_tier`/`is_chronically_absent` off the mart
  or Cube — functionally equivalent to Cube's tier logic for the `<=90%` case,
  but a literal `<0.795` threshold for "chronic tardy" doesn't match the ADA
  tier cutoffs (80%/90%) documented in Cube, so it may be tracking a distinct,
  undocumented business rule rather than a parity gap. Flagging as uncertain
  rather than asserting cube-coverage.
- Successful Calls Tracking (comm log) and CA Intervention Tracker
  (interventions) have **zero** Cube coverage — neither domain has a cube at
  all, only the two `rpt_tableau__*` extract models. These are the two true "no
  cube cube exists for this domain" gaps in the workbook, as opposed to "cube
  exists but doesn't expose this specific cut" gaps elsewhere.
- Did not fully trace `int_students__attendance_interventions` or
  `int_topline__attendance_contacts` SQL (out of scope per the "read as needed"
  instruction) — their rpt_ wrappers were sufficient to classify every
  measure/dimension sourced from them as mart-missing.
