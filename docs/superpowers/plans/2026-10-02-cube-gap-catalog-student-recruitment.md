  dashboard level).
- sheets excluded as hidden/scratch: 2 — `LP - Goups Roster` (a roster popup
  sheet, present in the workbook but placed on none of the 4 dashboards —
  confirmed by searching each `<dashboard>` block's zone names) and `Sheet 18`
  (same — not referenced by any dashboard's zones). The many `hidden='true'`
  `<windows>` entries (e.g. `SRE Team - Metrics`, `School Ops Team - Title`) are
  **not** excluded dashboards — they are individual worksheet panes hidden
  inside an in-scope dashboard's layout (titles/metrics tiles rendered via
  floating zones), so their fields are counted.
- regions served by this dashboard overall: **all** (Camden, Newark, Miami,
  Paterson). Verified two ways: (1) the `[region]` column's alias map defines
  all 4 KTAF regions with no workbook-level value restriction; (2)
  `User Filter 1 - Region` is a row-level-security filter keyed on Tableau group
  membership (`ISMEMBEROF('Group Staff NJ Regional')` → Camden/Newark/Paterson,
  `ISMEMBEROF('KNJ-SG-Tableau All Staff KTAF')` → all 4, Camden-only and
  Paterson-only staff groups, etc.) — it restricts which rows a given viewer
  sees, it does not hard-code the dashboard to a region subset. This is the
  opposite shape from the Attendance pilot's hard NJ-only filter.

### Notes

- All four Asana-listed rpt_/int_ models are confirmed present in the live
  workbook, including `rpt_tableau__fresh_dashboard_qc` (used on the Landing
  Page's QC Table/Roster sheets).
- **Blocker list is partly stale**: `dim_locations` and `dim_terms` already
  exist in `src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/dimensions/` (and `locations` is a live
  Cube cube, joined into several exposed views). `fct_anchor_metrics` and
  `fct_enrollment` still do **not** exist anywhere in `src/dbt/kipptaf/models/`
  — confirmed by directory search. So of the 4 named blockers, 2 are resolved
  and 2 remain.
- The Progress-to-Goals family is a combinatorial explosion of near-identical
  Tableau calcs (enrollment_type × status × snapshot-date × grain). I collapsed
  ~18 Sum-of-student_count variants into one measure row and ~8 target-distance
  variants into one row per the grouping instruction, rather than listing all
  ~30 raw calc names.
- "Display Value"/"Display Goal"/"Met Goal" wrap a real metric (funnel count or
  conversion rate vs. a goal) rather than being pure formatting, so they're kept
  as measures (flagged) even though they render as formatted strings.
- "School Level Int", "Counter" (INDEX()), and "Today" (TODAY()) are excluded as
  formatting/sort/tooltip helpers with no warehouse counterpart worth porting
  (`workbook-only`).
- Region classification as cube-covered is a judgment call: the Cube
  `regions`/`locations` cubes are generic KTAF reference dimensions exposed
  through unrelated domain views (course enrollment, attendance), not a
  FRESH/recruitment-specific view. The MEANING matches (same 4 regions); the
  domain doesn't. A future FRESH Cube view would still need to join in through
  `int_people__location_crosswalk` or an equivalent Finalsite-school crosswalk
  to use it directly.
- No Cube cube or view exists anywhere for Finalsite/recruitment-funnel data
  (inquiries, applications, offers, pending offers, conversion, recruitment
  goals). Every FRESH-specific measure is `mart-missing` today; none are
  `mart-ready` either, because the raw columns live in `extracts/tableau/`
  (int_/rpt_ models), not under `src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/`.
