About this round’s surveys

  • Support Survey: Your feedback will provide direction on how school operations teams and KTAF teams (HR Ops, Data, Technology) can improve this year and how they can incorporate your feedback in their planning for next year.
  • Manager Survey: This is where you provide feedback about your manager. It is prefilled based on the person you report to in ADP.
  • Staff School Community Diagnostic: This survey is administered to school-based teammates, along with parents and students later in the year.
  • Staff Info & Cert Update: This survey is always available for you to update demographic and certification information. If you’ve already completed it this school year, you do not have to complete it again.
