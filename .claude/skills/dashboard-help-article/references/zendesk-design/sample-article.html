Submitting a technology support request

Updated 2 days ago · KIPP NJ | Miami Technology
This article walks you through submitting a technology support request and tracking it to resolution.
Who this is for
All KIPP NJ and KIPP Miami staff.
Before you start
Sign in with your @kippnj.org account.
In this article
  • Submit a request
  • Track your ticket
  • Response times

Submit a request

  1. Go to help.kippnj.org and click Submit a request.
  2. Choose Technology from the request type list.
  3. Enter your school code in the Location field, for example NWK-RISE.
  4. Attach a screenshot. Press Shift + Cmd + 4 to capture a region.
  5. Click Submit. You will see a confirmation with your ticket number.
Note
Requests submitted after 4:00 PM are queued for the next business day.
The request form with Technology selected.

Track your ticket

Every request you submit appears under My activities, reachable from the avatar menu in the top right. You will also get an email each time the status changes.
What if I forgot my ticket number?
Open My activities from the avatar menu. Every request you have submitted is listed there with its number and status.
Warning
Replying to the notification email from a personal address creates a duplicate ticket. Always reply from your KIPP account.

Response times

Priority First response Example
Urgent 1 hour Whole-school network outage
High 4 hours Classroom projector down
Normal 2 business days Software install request
