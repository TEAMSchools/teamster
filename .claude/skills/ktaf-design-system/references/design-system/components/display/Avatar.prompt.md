Circular person chip showing a photo or initials; use for staff, teacher, and family contacts.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Aisha Brown" />
<Avatar src="/photo.jpg" name="Marcus Lee" size="lg" />
<AvatarGroup><Avatar name="A B"/><Avatar name="C D" tone="accent"/></AvatarGroup>
```

- `size`: `xs | sm | md | lg`; `tone`: `default | accent | indigo`.
- Photos render square inside the circle (no extra rounding). Use `AvatarGroup` to overlap.
