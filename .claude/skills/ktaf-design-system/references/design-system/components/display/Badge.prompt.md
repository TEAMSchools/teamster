Small all-caps pill for status or category; use for school grade bands, enrollment status, data freshness, etc.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>On track</Badge>
<Badge tone="indigo">K–4</Badge>
<Badge tone="accent">New</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `neutral | indigo | success | warning | danger | info | solid | accent`
- `dot` adds a leading status dot. Keep labels to 1–2 words.
