    KIPP Forward Data Suite (kipp_forward_data_suite)

    • workbook: KIPP Forward Data Suite | contentUrl KIPPForwardDataSuite_16988639234650 | luid ad72c094-ad35-48f4-86a0-9d2e5b53f59a (matches expected) | project Production
    • upstream datasources (all embedded, single-table extracts off rpt_ views): rpt_tableau__kfwd_dashboard, rpt_tableau__kfwd_spi, rpt_tableau__kfwd_persistence, rpt_tableau__kfwd_verification, rpt_tableau__kfwd_career_launch_survey, rpt_tableau__kfwd_contact_notes, rpt_tableau__kfwd_aid_report, rpt_gsheets__kfwd_rem_roster
    • rpt_/source models in use: the 8 above. rpt_tableau__kfwd_dashboard backs Completion, Match-Applications, Match-Placement Metrics, Splash Page, College Success, All Team-AS Metrics, and Grade Level Audit (it is the "mega" extract). Note: rpt_tableau__kfwd_career_launch_survey_weights and rpt_gsheets__kfwd_flc / _taf_contact_feed / _student_salesforce_ids exist in dbt under the same kfwd_ family but are not datasources of this workbook — don't fold them into this workbook's migration scope.
    • published dashboards inventoried (14, per get-workbook views, confirmed by matching dashboard/worksheet names in the downloaded .twb): Splash Page, Completion, All Team - AS Metrics, Verification Dashboard, KIPP Aid Report, Match- Placement Metrics, Match- Applications, Persistence, College Success, Career Launch, Re-Enrollment, HBCU Analysis, SPI, and Grade Level Audit (published as a standalone worksheet, not wrapped in a dashboard).
    • sheets excluded as hidden/scratch: 11 dashboards exist in the .twb but are NOT in the server's published view list (dev/legacy, no hidden flag on their <window> — they're simply not tabbed on the server): Board - Acceptance/Matriculation/Enrollment, Board - Acceptance/Matriculation/ Enrollment (2), Board Reports, Board: Acceptance, Board: Completion, Board: Enrollment, Board: Matriculation, Board: Persistence, Career Launch_OLD, Class of 2026 PostSec Stats, Dashboard 16. ~70 additional worksheets in the .twb are zone-children of those unpublished dashboards only and were excluded from field extraction.
    • regions served by this dashboard overall: not NJ-only. The workbook is hosted on Tableau's KIPPNJ site, but rpt_tableau__kfwd_dashboard.sql has no region where filter (population is ktc_status in ('HS9'..'TAFHS') network-wide) and includes an explicit KIPP Miami carve-out (c.contact_kipp_region_name = 'KIPP Miami' and c.ktc_status like 'TAF%', line 570). Every datasource also carries a kipp_region_name dimension and worksheets filter on a Regional User Filter calc (row-level permissioning, not a hard region scope). Treat as all regions (NJ regions + Miami) unless a school leader confirms Miami is excluded at the server/permission layer. This contradicts the task's "NJ alumni, possibly Miami" hint — Miami is in scope, not just possible.

    Cube coverage: none

    No cube or view under src/cube/model/ reads dim_colleges or dim_college_enrollments, and there is no postsecondary/alumni domain at all in Cube (only conformed, courses, staff, student_assessments, students). src/dbt/kipptaf/models/exposures/cube.yml lists both marts under cube_semantic_layer.depends_on (lines 17-18), but that is either aspirational or stale — no sql: in any cube/view file actually points at them. Every measure and dimension below is therefore cube-covered: none — the classification question is only mart-ready vs. mart-missing. I've dropped the cube-covered possibility from the status column narrative below and only call it out where relevant.

    Per the Asana task: fct_postsecondary_enrollment, fct_alumni_contact_notes, fct_survey_kfwd, dim_alumni, dim_institutions — confirmed absent (no matching file anywhere under src/dbt/kipptaf/models). dim_colleges and dim_college_enrollments do exist and cover a meaningful slice: college identity/selectivity/HBCU status (dim_colleges) and NSC enrollment status/degree/major/class-level (dim_college_enrollments) — see mart-ready rows below.

    Notes

    • Biggest finding: there is no postsecondary/alumni Cube domain at all, so this workbook's entire measure set is mart-missing or workbook-only — there are zero cube-covered rows. The Asana task's blocker list is accurate; the gap is total, not partial.
    • dim_colleges and dim_college_enrollments are real, usable building blocks today for the college-identity and raw-NSC-status slice (HBCU flag, selectivity, state, degree/class-level/graduated/withdrawn), but they cover maybe 10-15% of this workbook's measure surface. The other ~85% (advising touchpoints, application/acceptance/matriculation tracking, ECC-window grad rates, financial aid, career-readiness survey, re-enrollment) has no dbt mart today — it all lives as bespoke SQL inside the 7 rpt_tableau__kfwd_* / 1 rpt_gsheets__kfwd_* extracts.
    • Region scope is a correction to the task's hint: the workbook is not NJ-only. rpt_tableau__kfwd_dashboard.sql has no region where clause and explicitly carves out KIPP Miami contacts; treat Miami as in-scope for any migration plan, not "possibly."
    • cube.yml's cube_semantic_layer.depends_on lists dim_colleges and dim_college_enrollments even though no cube/view reads them — flag this to whoever owns that exposure file; it's either a forward-declared TODO or drift.
    • Grouping judgment calls: the "As1-As24 / cc1-cc5" family, the "Is Xyr [deg] Grad Int" family, and the Match- Applications/Placement application-outcome family are each 15-45 near-identical per-cohort-window or per-degree-type variants of one underlying metric shape; I collapsed each into one row rather than listing every variant, per the grouping instruction.
    • Excluded as pure display/layout: MIN/MAX(1) placeholders, index() sort keys, View By - * parameters, string-literal "Title" labels, and the COUNT(...)/20 and COUNT(...)/40 goal-denominator helpers on Re-Enrollment (these ARE workbook-only despite being numeric — they're fixed constants used only to compute a bar-chart percent-of-goal, not a standalone metric).
    • rpt_tableau__kfwd_career_launch_survey_weights is a sibling extract in dbt's kfwd_ family but is not wired into this workbook (not in upstreamDatasources) — don't assume it needs migrating alongside this dashboard.
