This is the opposite shape from the Attendance pilot's hard NJ-only filter.

    Notes

    • All four Asana-listed rpt_/int_ models are confirmed present in the live workbook, including rpt_tableau__fresh_dashboard_qc (used on the Landing Page's QC Table/Roster sheets).
    • Blocker list is partly stale: dim_locations and dim_terms already exist in src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/dimensions/ (and locations is a live Cube cube, joined into several exposed views). fct_anchor_metrics and fct_enrollment still do not exist anywhere in src/dbt/kipptaf/models/ — confirmed by directory search. So of the 4 named blockers, 2 are resolved and 2 remain.
    • The Progress-to-Goals family is a combinatorial explosion of near-identical Tableau calcs (enrollment_type × status × snapshot-date × grain). I collapsed ~18 Sum-of-student_count variants into one measure row and ~8 target-distance variants into one row per the grouping instruction, rather than listing all ~30 raw calc names.
    • "Display Value"/"Display Goal"/"Met Goal" wrap a real metric (funnel count or conversion rate vs. a goal) rather than being pure formatting, so they're kept as measures (flagged) even though they render as formatted strings.
    • "School Level Int", "Counter" (INDEX()), and "Today" (TODAY()) are excluded as formatting/sort/tooltip helpers with no warehouse counterpart worth porting (workbook-only).
    • Region classification as cube-covered is a judgment call: the Cube regions/locations cubes are generic KTAF reference dimensions exposed through unrelated domain views (course enrollment, attendance), not a FRESH/recruitment-specific view. The MEANING matches (same 4 regions); the domain doesn't. A future FRESH Cube view would still need to join in through int_people__location_crosswalk or an equivalent Finalsite-school crosswalk to use it directly.
    • No Cube cube or view exists anywhere for Finalsite/recruitment-funnel data (inquiries, applications, offers, pending offers, conversion, recruitment goals). Every FRESH-specific measure is mart-missing today; none are mart-ready either, because the raw columns live in extracts/tableau/ (int_/rpt_ models), not under src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/.
