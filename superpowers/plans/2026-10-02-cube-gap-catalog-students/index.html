    Notes

    • Grain mismatch is the main judgment call. Both in-scope dashboards are row-level QA worklists (which specific student/teacher/module has a problem), not aggregate reporting. Cube is built for aggregate, access-controlled analytics — so even where the underlying raw column is cube-covered (student name, school, assessment title, percent_correct), the dashboard's actual job (drill to the offending row and fix it in the source system) isn't something a Cube view does today. Treat "cube-covered" here as "the attribute exists in Cube," not "Cube can replace this dashboard."
    • dim_students decodes mask nulls. race always resolves to a label ('Not Listed' fallback) and can't reproduce the "Missing Ethnicity" / "Missing Race/Ethnicity" raw-null checks — this is the opposite of a pattern-match "name doesn't need to match" case; the VALUES actually diverge (decoded non-null vs raw null). Confirmed by reading dim_students.sql directly rather than assuming the Cube race dimension covers it. gender_identity and birth_date ARE raw passthroughs (no coalesce), so those two are legitimately mart-ready.
    • Three dashboards in the .twb are not live. "RC Comment Audit" explicitly says it moved to GradebookDashboard/Appendix-CommentAudit (another workbook) — that functionality should not be scoped into this dashboard's replacement at all. "Assessment Audit - Zero-Answered" has no worksheet zone of its own (pure instructional text + a drill-through target), so it carries no metrics to inventory. "(Archived)" is a stale duplicate.
