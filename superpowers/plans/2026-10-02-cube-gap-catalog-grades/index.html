    Notes

    • Grouping: the 9 "Y1 Landing - BAN *" sheets (HS/MS/Network × <2.0 / Failing≥2 / ≥3.0) share byte-identical formulas across all 9 — they differ only in the Grade-Level filter value and (for Network) the absence of a grade filter. Collapsed into 2 rows above ("Y1 threshold rate" and "week-over-week change") rather than listing 9x.
    • Grouping: "LP - Tile *" and "LP - Strip *" pairs (4 pairs: Course Failures, Cumulative GPA, Gradebook Health, Y1 GPA) are the same measures rendered as a KPI tile vs. a per-region strip — not separately listed.
    • Excluded from measures/dimensions entirely as pure layout/navigation helpers with no data content: title sheets (LP - Title, GPA - Title, Y1 Landing - Title, Y1 Schools - Title, Teacher title, School rollup title), guide/ instruction sheets (LP - Guide *, DL Instructions link), and UI-only controls (Panel close button, Links - GPA Roster - * drill-through buttons, Teacher sections panel eyebrow).
    • GPA Goals - Y1 is NOT a 6th distinct data source despite its own caption — it's a second embedded instance of the exact same rpt_tableau__gpa_goals BigQuery table under a cosmetic rename. Don't double-count it when scoping Cube work.
    • The Asana task's blocker list (rpt_tableau__assignment_checks, rpt_tableau__gradebook_gpa, rpt_tableau__gradebook_gpa_cumulative, rpt_tableau__gradebook_es_comments, rpt_tableau__gradebook_ms_hs_comments) does not match what this live workbook actually reads (see header). Possible explanations not investigated further (out of scope): the Asana task describes a planned future state, an older workbook version, or a naming drift between what Asana calls these models and their current names — flagging for the task owner rather than guessing.
    • Judgment call: classified gradebook-audit's health/flag measures as mart-missing even though fct_grades_assignments ships adjacent assignment-level flags (is_missing, is_late, is_exempt, is_expected, is_counted_in_final_grade) — because no mart currently aggregates those to the section/category grain compared against an expectation count. That aggregation + the expectation-count logic itself (int_powerschool__u_expectations_qtd_unpivot) has no mart presence today.
    • PII caution honored throughout: no row-level values, student names, or counts from query results appear above — only field names, model paths, and trimmed formulas, per the task's Step 0 ground rules and .claude/rules/ferpa-pii.md.
