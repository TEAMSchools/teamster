    Leader intent to return survey: design

    Issue: #6251

    Why

    Recruitment runs a separate intent to return survey for leaders. Until now the only intent to return survey went to every active staff member outside the KTAF business unit, and who got it was written into the code. Last year that left out Teaching and Learning, a KTAF department whose leaders wanted to take it. They filled in a different form offline, and those answers never reached the warehouse.

    This year recruitment chose the leader population themselves, through a setup page, and sent their rules on 2026-11-17. The build turns those rules into survey assignments, completion tracking, a reminder list, and a follow-up export.

    Goals

    • Leaders get the leader survey and only the leader survey. Nobody gets both intent to return surveys, and nobody gets both sets of reminders.
    • Leader completion shows on the existing survey dashboard, separate from staff intent to return completion.
    • Recruitment gets a Google Sheet listing leaders who haven't finished, with each leader's manager. Their Zapier reminder reads it, and recruitment's follow-up owner works from it to follow up one at a time.
    • The leader rule lives in one model that every consumer reads.

    Non-goals

    • A config sheet for the population. The rules change once a year and are hard-coded from recruitment's submission.
    • Adding intent to return to bridge_survey_expectations (Asana task 1219288212398597). The Survey Dashboard tracks completion from rpt_tableau__survey_links and rpt_tableau__survey_completion; only Cube reads the bridge.
    • Building or editing the Google Form, the reporting__terms row, or the Zap in code. Those are setup steps (below).

    Anti-goals

    These would make the build a failure even if it runs:

    • A leader appears in both the leader and the staff intent to return populations.
    • Anyone outside the leader population drops out of, or newly enters, the staff intent to return population.
    • The existing general reminder feed changes shape. Its Sheet feeds a live Zap.
    • A leader response counts as a staff intent to return response, or the reverse.

    33 people hold a listed title in a department that is not listed, 32 of them at KTAF (Directors and Managing Directors in Teacher Development, Talent Acquisition, HR, Data, and similar). Recruitment confirmed on 2026-11-20 that they stay out. Because KTAF also gets no staff intent to return survey, they get no intent to return survey at all. That is intended.

    The staff intent to return population stays as it is today, everyone eligible outside KTAF, minus anyone in the leader population.

    Setup outside the code (owner: data team, before the build is verified)

    1. Create the leader Google Form as a copy of the staff intent to return form. Title it exactly Leader Intent to Return Survey. Replace the main intent to return question with recruitment's leader question and its 4 answers, with the "LEADERS:" prefix removed from the question. Save it in the surveys Drive folder that google_forms_responses_sensor scans.
    2. Add a reporting__terms row: academic year 2026, name Leader Intent to Return Survey, code LITR, type SURVEY, start 2026-11-30, end 2026-12-11.
    3. Create the follow-up Google Sheet connected to the new extract, and share it with recruitment.
    4. Recruitment points their Zap at that Sheet. Recruitment's reminder text is in the setup page's store, with "[deadline]" replaced by Friday, December 11.

    The form title must equal the terms row name: int_surveys__survey_submissions attributes a response to a round only when the form title matches the terms row name and the submit date falls inside its dates. The LITR code keeps leader responses apart from staff responses, which use ITR, because completion joins on the round code.

    The build (decided)

    1. New domain model int_surveys__leader_intent_to_return_population in models/surveys/intermediate/, at one row per employee. It applies the survey eligibility and recruitment's 3 lists. This is the only place the rule lives.
    2. rpt_tableau__survey_links:
    3. New union branch, survey Leader Intent to Return Survey, joined to the reporting__terms row by that name, reading its people from the new population model. The link is the new form's responder URL.
    4. The staff Intent to Return Survey branch excludes anyone in the population model, for academic year 2026 and later only. Past rounds keep their original populations, so historic completion does not change.
    5. rpt_tableau__survey_completion needs no change. It picks up the new branch and matches responses on survey_round = survey_code.
    6. rpt_extracts__survey_reminder leaves out rows for Leader Intent to Return Survey. Leaders still get general reminders for their other surveys. Its output stays one email column.
    7. New extract in models/extracts/google/sheets/: one row per leader in the current leader round who has not submitted. Columns: name, job title, department, business unit, location, work email, manager name, manager email. It reads the population model and int_surveys__survey_submissions, not another rpt_ (A1). Staff name and email columns carry contains_pii: true. A submission counts when it matches the current academic year and term code LITR, which means inside the terms row's dates. A late answer counts only if the end date is extended. The Sheet is a full refresh: the Zap reads every row, and recruitment keeps its own notes outside it.
    8. A Google Sheets exposure for the new extract, with the Sheet's URL.
    9. rpt_appsheet__seat_tracker_roster.itr_response reads leader answers too, so leaders don't show blank in the seat tracker. It maps the leader question (by its question_shortname on the new form) like this:
    10. "This is my last school year in my current role…" → Not Returning
    11. "Committed through SY 27-28…" → Returning 1 Year
    12. "Committed through SY 28-29…" → Returning 2 Years
    13. "Committed for the next 2+ years" → Returning 2+ Years

    The 3 Returning values are new to itr_response. Check the AppSheet column and anything else that reads it for a fixed list of allowed values.

    1. Add Leader Intent to Return Survey to the survey title filter in rpt_tableau__survey_responses, so leader answers reach the dashboard.

    Left to the builder

    • Model names, apart from the survey title and term code above, which the form and the sheet must match.
    • Whether the follow-up extract reads dim_staff or int_people__staff_roster for names and managers. Follow A2: use the mart if it has the columns.
    • A check before merge: the staff branch of rpt_tableau__survey_links on the PR matches prod minus the leader population, person for person.
    • Tests. At minimum: a uniqueness test on each new model's grain, and a unit test on the population rule covering a person who matches all 3 lists, one who matches only 2, and a KTAF Teaching and Learning leader.
    • Whether the survey dashboard needs any change to show the new survey, for example a hard-coded survey filter. Check the workbook.

    Open with recruitment

    • Whether removing Chief Academic Officer from the titles was intentional.

    Constraints

    • The survey opens 2026-11-30, so the change has to merge and deploy before then.
    • The form's responder URL exists only after step 1 of the setup. The build can start before that, but the link can't be filled in until then.

    Revision 2026-11-20: no new-hire wait for intent to return

    Decided during final review. Neither intent to return survey waits 3 weeks after a hire or rehire; both ask about next year, which a new hire is also planning. Leader eligibility is active plus primary assignment. The staff intent to return survey gains recent hires outside KTAF, which supersedes the anti-goal that nobody newly enters the staff population. Every other survey keeps the 3-week wait.

    Revision 2026-11-20: transfer and not-sure answers for leaders

    Recruitment added 2 answers to the leader question and a transfer section copied from the staff form. The seat tracker maps the leader transfer answer to Interested in Transfer and the leader not-sure answer to Not Sure, alongside the 4 mappings in build step 7. Matching uses the wording before any network name, so the KIPP South Florida rebrand in the answers doesn't break it.

    Revision 2026-11-20: Connected Sheet instead of extracts

    Recruitment's reminder list and responses view are one Connected Sheet with custom queries, so it could exist before this change deploys. The reminder tab reads rpt_tableau__survey_completion (leader survey, open round, completion 0); the responses tab reads rpt_tableau__survey_responses (latest submission per person), which shows a round's answers only after the round closes. Build steps 5 and 6 are replaced by one exposure for that Sheet; no new extract model ships.

    Revision 2026-11-20: leader rule inline in survey links

    At review, the leader rule moved from its own intermediate model into a leader_population CTE in rpt_tableau__survey_links, so this change touches only report models. The CTE reads eligible_roster, and both the leader branch and the staff intent to return exclusion join it. Moving eligibility rules into a domain intermediate model for bridge_survey_expectations is tracked in
