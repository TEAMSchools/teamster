    Region-aware roll-up for internal assessments

    Closes #6308.

    Intent

    Internal Illuminate assessments that carry the same tags (school year, scope, subject, module code, grade) roll up into one group. That is by design: it is how the parts of a multi-part assessment, and alternate versions, become one result per student.

    The region tag (regions_assessed) was added after the roll-up was built, and the roll-up never took it into account. When Camden and Newark each have their own copy of a module, both copies land in one group, and the copy with the lowest assessment_id supplies the group's title, date, band set and report card tags for every region. The region whose copy was not created first then shows:

    • another region's title and date, so its scores sit in the wrong week on the DDI weekly views and the wrong quarter under the Term filter;
    • a section lookup on a date the student did not sit the test, which drops a small number of scores from the scores fact;
    • another region's report card and progress report tags, which today leaves Camden's grade 1 and 2 literacy assessments off Camden's feeds;
    • another region's performance band set (see Open items).

    The Data Team decided that the whole roll-up becomes region-aware, not only the date: two copies combine for a region only when both are tagged for that region.

    Decisions

    Decision Source
    The roll-up is regional. A region's group is every identically tagged copy whose regions_assessed lists that region. A copy tagged for several regions sits in each of those regions' groups. Decided 2026-11-12
    A regional group's ID is the lowest assessment_id among the copies tagged for that region. Decided 2026-11-12
    Rejected: grouping copies only when their region lists match exactly. It splits one combined score in two for about 2,500 student-assessments in SY25-26 and 1,400 in SY26-27. Decided 2026-11-12
    The Assessment Dashboard shows one row per regional assessment. No workbook change ships with this. Decided 2026-11-12
    Scores whose section cannot be resolved are the resolver working as designed. No resolver fallback, no "first copy with sittings" rule, no clean-up of old-year dates, and no anchor-count acceptance check. Decided 2026-11-12
    Replacement scores missing from the scores fact get their own issue. Issue #6308

    Goals

    • Two copies combine for a region only when both are tagged for that region.
    • Each region's scores carry the title, date, and report card and progress report tags of that region's own first copy.
    • No student's combined score changes: the set of copies rolled into each student's result, and its percent correct, are the same before and after.
    • Every key derived from the group ID is hashed from the same ID and date by its producer and all of its consumers.

    Non-goals

    • Correcting tag errors, such as a WPP2 copy tagged WPP 1. Those are on the tagging work list and are fixed in the tagging app.
    • Changing the section resolver or the scaffold's course enrollment join (between), including making it half-open.
    • The replacement-score gap.
    • Removing is_florida_assessment from the tag grouping. Miami-only copies already group apart, and their group IDs do not change under this design.
    • Tableau workbook changes.
    • An override for Camden's Literacy G1 UA2, which moves to Camden's Q2 because Camden gave it on November 9, 2026.

    Anti-goals

    • A student's combined score splitting into two results.
    • One column name, canonical_assessment_id, carrying two meanings (the tag group on one model, the regional group on another).
    • A producer and a consumer hashing different group IDs or dates. No mart test catches an orphaned key.

    Constraints

    • A5: int_assessments__score_anchors holds an existing row_number() = 1 pick. Do not edit inside it. It needs no edit, because its date comes from the canonical model by group ID.
    • Hash-change discipline (src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/CLAUDE.md): the group ID and date are hash inputs to assessment_administration_key and to the score key. Producer and every consumer change in one commit.
    • Key churn is unavoidable for the regions that get a new group ID. Issue #6308 asked to keep c.administered_date as the hash input to avoid churn; that held for a date-only fix and does not hold here.
    • The assessment intermediates and marts are tables on one cron tick. Cube's proficiency_rollup pre-aggregation follows the scores fact's rebuild only for partitions from July 1 of the previous school year onward. Older partitions, which hold SY24-25's 12 split groups, rebuild only by hand in Cube Cloud.
    • Never bare dbt; always uv run.

    Approaches

    1. A per-region date only. Keep one group per tag set and give each region its own date. Smallest change and no key churn beyond the date. It leaves the title, band set and report card tags coming from another region's copy. Superseded by the decision above.
    2. A regional group ID from a copy-by-region crosswalk (chosen). Keep the members model at one row per copy. Add one model at one row per copy and region that carries the regional group ID. Every consumer that needs a group reads it there or from the scaffold, which already works at that grain.
    3. Change the members model's grain to copy by region. One model instead of two, but every copy-grain consumer (rpt_appsheet__assessments, the two tag audits, dim_assessments, the band model) would have to collapse it again.

    Design

    Terms

    • Tag group: copies sharing source, internal flag, Florida flag, school year, scope, subject, module code and grade. This is today's grouping, unchanged. Its ID is the lowest assessment_id in it.
    • Regional group: within a tag group, the copies tagged for one region. Its ID is the lowest assessment_id among them.

    When a tag group's first copy is tagged for a region, that region's regional group ID equals the tag group ID, so nothing changes for it.

    1. int_assessments__assessments_members

    Grain unchanged: one row per source and assessment_id.

    Rename canonical_assessment_id to tag_group_assessment_id. The values do not change. The rename makes every reader of the old column fail to compile until it is pointed at the right group, which is the point: a reader left on the tag group silently keeps the cross-region roll-up.

    2. New int_assessments__assessment_region_groups

    A build model (a crosswalk) at one row per source, assessment_id and region, for internal assessments. It is the one place the regional rule lives.

    select
    m.source,
    m.assessment_id,
    m.tag_group_assessment_id,

    region,

    min(m.assessment_id) over (
        partition by m.source, m.tag_group_assessment_id, region
    ) as canonical_assessment_id,
from {{ ref("int_assessments__assessments_members") }} as m
cross join unnest(m.regions_assessed_array) as region
where m.is_internal_assessment

    Apex rows pass through unchanged: each is its own tag group with region Miami. Uniqueness test on source, assessment_id and region.

    3. int_assessments__assessments_canonical

    Grain unchanged: one row per source and canonical_assessment_id. The set of IDs becomes every regional group ID from the crosswalk plus every tag group ID. A tag group ID that is no region's group (its first copy has no region tagged) keeps its row with an empty regions_array, so the replacement fallback below still finds a title and date.

    • Attributes (title, date, subject, scope, module code, year, grade) come from the copy whose assessment_id is the group ID. The row is determined, so the dbt_utils.deduplicate pick is no longer needed.
    • regions_array becomes the regions whose regional group this ID is. Today it is every region tagged anywhere in the tag group.
    • Add tag_group_assessment_id.

    dim_assessment_administrations reads this model fanned by regions_array. Its SQL does not change, and it now yields one administration per regional group and region, dated by that region's first copy.

    4. int_assessments__scaffold

    • assessment_region_scaffold: join the crosswalk in place of the unnest, and take canonical_assessment_id from it. Students are already rostered only to copies tagged for their region, so rows and rostering do not change.
    • Replacement branch: the student's region comes from their school. Look up the regional group for the copy's tag group and that region in the canonical model. When one exists, use its ID and set is_region_tagged true. When none exists, fall back to tag_group_assessment_id and set it false, which matches today's value.
    • Non-internal and Apex branches: canonical_assessment_id is the copy's own tag_group_assessment_id. Values do not change.

    The score anchors, section resolver and scores fact read the group ID from the scaffold and the date from the canonical model, so they follow without logic changes, apart from the joins named below. The response roll-up follows for the group ID, title and date; its band lookup changes as described next.

    5. int_assessments__performance_bands and the roll-up's band lookup

    Depends on open item 1. As recommended there, only the band set becomes regional:

    • Rule. A score is labeled from the band set on its regional group's first copy. For a standard or reporting group, that is the first copy in the group that carries it. A replacement row uses the group the scaffold assigned it.
    • The standard merge stays on the tag group. canonical_response_type_id keeps its window over the tag group. It is only a representative ID for the same standard across copies. Making it regional could change response_type_id, and with it the score key, for regions whose group ID does not change.
    • The roll-up's join moves. int_assessments__response_rollup joins the band model in responses_keyed, on single sittings that carry no group yet, and aggregates on the band set before the scaffold join. The band set lookup has to happen once the group is known. rpt_deanslist__mod_standards reads the band set through the roll-up's group ID and follows the same rule.

    If the decision goes the other way, the band model only swaps canonical_assessment_id for tag_group_assessment_id and nothing else moves.

    6. Marts

    • fct_assessment_scores_enrollment_scoped: the roll-up's assessment_id is the group ID. Join the canonical model on it directly instead of through the member's own group column. Raw academic_year and module_code still come from the member row for that ID.
    • bridge_assessment_administration_members is disabled, so it does not compile and the rename will not flag it. Update its SQL to read copy, region and group ID from the crosswalk anyway, so it is right if it is re-enabled. It stays disabled and is not part of verification.
    • bridge_assessment_expectations_student_scoped and bridge_assessment_expectations_enrollment_scoped: take the group ID from the scaffold row, not from the member's own group column.
    • dim_assessments: member grain, and its key does not hash the group ID, so no key changes. It joins the canonical model only for the grade, which is the same for every copy in a tag group.

    Key effects: assessment_administration_key and the score key change for rows whose regional group ID differs from today's group ID. assessment_key on the fact moves to the regional first copy's existing dim_assessments row. No column is added to or removed from a mart, so the Cube model files need no edit.

    7. One reader outside the assessment star

    int_assessments__college_assessment_practice (CARAT practice SAT and ACT scores) joins the roll-up's group ID to a hand-kept conversion sheet. A practice test in a group that splits would need its new ID on the sheet. None of the 11 practice IDs sits in a split group today (checked 2026-11-12). No change is needed; verification re-checks it.

    8. After merge

    • All changed tables rebuild in one pass so keys agree.
    • The Data Team rebuilds the proficiency_rollup partitions before July 1, 2025 by hand in Cube Cloud, so SY24-25 picks up its new keys and dates.

    9. Tests, docs and skill

    • Update int_assessments__assessments_members__no_cross_source_canonical, int_assessments__scaffold__replacement_region_untagged and the properties files that name the renamed column.
    • docs/models/ddi-stack-data-model.md: replace the decision "Canonical grouping by attributes, not region" and the terms that describe the group.
    • .claude/skills/ddi-stack/references/tagging.md: rewrite "Canonical grouping", including the Florida note. report-cards.md: the gate now reads the tags on the region's own first copy.

The full list is on the Regional dates tab of the tagging work list (Google Sheet "DDI assessment tagging fixes (SY25-26 and SY26-27)").

    Verification

    1. Combined scores do not change. For internal, non-replacement rows of the response roll-up, compare the branch build to prod on student, the sorted list of member copies (assessment_ids) and response type. Row counts and percent correct match exactly.
    2. Only the expected rows get a new group ID. Rows whose group ID differs from prod fall only in the tag groups listed above, and each row's region is tagged on its new first copy.
    3. Keys resolve. Every internal assessment_administration_key on the scores fact and on the two expectation bridges exists in dim_assessment_administrations. Check counts, not compilation. For rows whose group ID does not change, the score key matches prod.
    4. Report cards. Camden's grade 2 literacy domain rows appear for the students who lack them today (172 on 2026-11-12), and grade 1 and 2 literacy progress report rows appear (141 and 173). A replay of the feed logic matched the published feed exactly, so the same replay against the branch build is the check.
    5. Dates. Literacy G3 ET3 (SY25-26): Newark and Paterson scores carry January 28, 2026, and Camden's carry September 22, 2025.
    6. Uniqueness tests pass on the crosswalk and on every model whose grain is named above.
    7. CARAT. No practice SAT or ACT ID on the conversion sheet sits in a group that splits.
    8. Bands, if open item 1 is yes. Mastery changes only in the modules listed there, and replacement rows keep a band set.

    Left to the builder

    • The final name of tag_group_assessment_id and of the crosswalk model.
    • Whether the crosswalk needs a grain-projection distinct for a region typed twice in one tag, and how the empty-string region that a trailing comma produces is carried. Keep today's behavior for both.
    • The exact join shape for the replacement branch's regional lookup.
    • Whether dim_assessments keeps its canonical join or reads the grade from the member row.
    • The rebuild after merge: all changed tables must rebuild in one pass so keys agree. Confirm how the deploy triggers that, per src/dbt/kipptaf/models/marts/CLAUDE.md.
    • Follow-up issue for the replacement-score gap.

    Open items

    1. Should performance bands follow the regional group? Today the tag group's first copy supplies the band set for everyone. In 11 of the 52 split groups the regional copies carry different band sets, and in 9 of those the cut points differ. If bands become regional, 130 overall results change mastery: 67 in SY26-27 (Newark Chemistry ET 1, which today is judged by a Camden-only copy nobody sat) and 63 in SY25-26 across four literacy modules. Standard-level results were not measured. Recommended: yes, because the decision was that the whole roll-up is regional, and a region should be judged by the cut points on the test it sat. Two copies carry Illuminate's default band set (Camden's Literacy G4 ET1 and Newark's Literacy G7 ET1, both SY25-26), which looks like a setup miss; under this option those regions would be judged by the default set until the copy is corrected. This contradicts the stakeholder doc's line that no mastery result changes, so the doc changes with the answer.
    2. Camden Literacy G1 UA2 lands in Q2. No override is planned. Teaching & Learning has not yet confirmed the quarter.

    Approval, 2026-11-12

    Approved by the Data Team on 2026-11-12, with open item 1 decided:

    • Performance bands follow the regional group. Section 5 is built as written: the band set becomes regional and the standard merge stays on the tag group.
    • It may be rolled back. The decision is flagged to stakeholders as a question in the stakeholder doc. Build the band change as its own commit, after the group change, so it can be reverted without touching the regional grouping. Reverted, the band model reads tag_group_assessment_id and labels do not change from today.

    Open item 2 stays open and does not block the build.

    Revision, 2026-11-12: built inside existing models

    The PR's new owner changed the design before build. Where this section and the sections above disagree, this section wins.

    • Region is part of the canonical definition. canonical_assessment_id keeps its name and now groups per region. No tag_group_assessment_id. Grouping exists only to merge the parts of a multi-part assessment; regional variants are distinct assessments and never merge across regions.
    • No new model. int_assessments__assessments_members carries region_groups, an array of (region, canonical_assessment_id) per copy. Consumers unnest it. Section 2's int_assessments__assessment_region_groups is not built.
    • No fallback for untagged regions. A replacement score from a region the copy is not tagged for drops in the scaffold. The replacement_region_untagged warn test and is_region_tagged are not built.
    • Bands and standards both follow the regional group. int_assessments__performance_bands adds canonical_assessment_id to its grain and merges per group, so each region keeps its own band set. The band change ships in the same PR, not as a separate revertible commit.
    • dim_assessments grade comes from each copy's own grade_level_id, not from the canonical row.
