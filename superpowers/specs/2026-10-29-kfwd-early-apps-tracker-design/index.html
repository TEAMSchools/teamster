    KIPP Forward early apps tracker: design

    Issue: #6190

    Intent

    KIPP Forward counselors and school leaders track whether seniors who plan to apply early (Early Action, EA, or Early Decision, ED) are meeting the early-application goals set for their GPA band. Today the tracker is a one-time spreadsheet built from 2 Overgrad exports. A student who submits after an export never appears, and a stakeholder found 2 seniors with GPAs above 3.75 and submitted ED applications missing for exactly that reason.

    The goal is a Google Sheet that refreshes every day from Overgrad data in BigQuery, so stakeholders always see who is on track, who has not submitted yet, and who just missed a goal. Counselors use it to decide which seniors to follow up with before early deadlines.

    Goals

    • A Reports sheet with 4 tabs: Summary, Roster, Not Submitted Yet, Near Misses.
    • Data refreshes daily with no manual export.
    • Every rule lives in dbt, so a rule change is one code change.
    • The sheet rolls to the next senior class each July without edits.

    Non-goals

    • "Meets full need" as a real college attribute. It stays a placeholder that treats every college as meeting full need, until the source is cleaned up.
    • Near misses on GPA (for example a 3.73 just under the 3.75 bar).
    • Counting Will Apply applications toward any criterion.
    • Changing the Tableau KIPP Forward Data Suite. A separate Tableau sheet with these criteria on Salesforce data exists only as a review copy.

    Anti-goals

    • A tracker that disagrees with Overgrad for longer than one refresh. That is the failure being fixed.
    • Rule logic in spreadsheet formulas. The report sheet uses IMPORTRANGE only (docs/guides/google-sheets.md).
    • Stakeholders holding the source sheet link, where an edit can break the refresh for everyone.

    Constraints

    • Two-sheet publishing. Per docs/guides/google-sheets.md: a source sheet in the Data Integration shared drive's IMPORTRANGE Sources folder, named exactly after the model, holds the Connected Sheets extracts and the refresh schedule. A Reports sheet with friendly tab names pulls each tab with IMPORTRANGE. The exposure url points at the source sheet.
    • Layering. Rule A1 in .claude/rules/dbt-architecture.md: an rpt_ reads marts and domain int_ models only. rpt_gsheets__overgrad_award_letter reads Overgrad staging directly, but that edge is a baselined exception (src/dbt/kipptaf/standard-baseline.tsv, #5811) and must not be copied. The per-student logic goes in a domain int_ (folders listed in DOMAIN_FOLDERS, scripts/check_dbt_standard.py).
    • PII. The roster, not-submitted, and near-miss models carry student names, GPAs, and application status. Tag them contains_pii per .claude/rules/ferpa-pii.md. The Summary model is aggregate only.
    • Naming. New KIPP Forward Google Sheets extracts use the rpt_gsheets__kfwd_ prefix (src/dbt/kipptaf/CLAUDE.md).

    Approaches

    • A. dbt models feeding the two-sheet Connected Sheets setup. Chosen. All rules live in reviewed, tested SQL, and a counselor cannot break the tracker by editing it. It costs a code review and about 30 minutes of manual sheet setup, and every later column change must be made in both sheets.
    • B. Connected Sheets on the raw Overgrad tables with the rules as sheet formulas. It could go live the same day. It was rejected because the near-miss and "additional school" rules would be long, untested formulas that break when someone sorts or inserts a column, and because it violates the team's no-logic-in-report-sheets rule.
    • Dropped: the Tableau sheet as the tracker (stakeholders want a Google Sheet, and it reads Salesforce, not Overgrad), and a Dagster asset that writes rows into the sheet (no such pattern exists; published sheets read BigQuery through Connected Sheets).

    Decisions

    Source

    Overgrad directly, not Salesforce. Overgrad syncs to Salesforce about 1 day behind, and the stakeholder wants the freshest data. The Tableau dashboards read Salesforce, so on a given day the tracker may be 1 day ahead of them.

    Population (N)

    • Current seniors: Overgrad int_overgrad__students.graduation_year = var("current_academic_year") + 1.
    • High schools: KIPP Cooper Norcross High School, KIPP Newark Collegiate Academy, KIPP Newark Lab High School (school__name). The Overgrad "MS Completers" schools are excluded.
    • N = seniors whose student-level custom field is_ed_ea = Yes. On 2026-10-29 that was 130 seniors.
    • GPA = academics__unweighted_gpa. Bands: 3.75+, 3.50-3.74, 3.00-3.49, below 3.00. A flagged senior below 3.00, or with no GPA, is in N and shows N/A for every criterion.
    • Counselor = assigned_counselor__first_name and assigned_counselor__last_name.

    Applications

    From int_overgrad__admissions, joined to the student on student__id and _dbt_source_project. Both models union regions, and ids can repeat across regions (rule A10).

    • Counts are distinct colleges (university__id), not admission rows, so a duplicate row can never turn a No into a Yes. On 2026-10-29 no current senior had 2 submitted early rows for one college.

    • GPA band uses round(academics__unweighted_gpa, 2), matching the KIPP Forward Tableau logic. Overgrad stores 2 decimals or fewer today, so the rounding changes nothing now.

    • Early: due_date__type in early_action, early_action_2, early_decision, early_decision_2, single_choice_early_action. regular, priority, and null are not early.

    • ED: early_decision, early_decision_2. EA: early_action, early_action_2, single_choice_early_action.
    • Submitted: status in Applied, Accepted, Denied, Deferred, Waitlisted, Enrolled. Not submitted: Will Apply, Withdrew, Did Not Apply.
    • The admissions-level custom field is_ed_ea is blank on every current-senior row. Do not use it.

    URM graduation rate

    Overgrad does not carry graduation rates. Use stg_kippadb__account.adjusted_6_year_minority_graduation_rate, joined on stg_kippadb__account.nces_id = cast(int_overgrad__admissions.university__ipeds_id as string). This is the rate the Tableau dashboards use. On 2026-10-29 it matched 986 of 991 current-senior early applications, with no IPEDS id on more than 1 account. An application with no rate never counts toward a URM bar and is never a near miss.

    Criteria

    Each criterion applies only to its GPA band. Every count uses submitted early applications only.

    Band Criterion Rule
    3.75+ 1 ED, full need, 80%+ URM At least 1 submitted ED application with URM >= 80 at a full-need college
    3.75+ 2 EA, 78%+ URM At least 2 submitted EA applications with URM >= 78
    3.75+ Met both Both rows above
    3.50-3.74 1 ED/EA, full need, 80%+ URM At least 1 submitted early application with URM >= 80 at a full-need college
    3.50-3.74 2 additional early, 68%+ URM (Submitted early applications with URM >= 68) minus min(full-need 80%+ count, 1) >= 2
    3.50-3.74 Met both Both rows above
    3.00-3.49 3 early, 68%+ URM At least 3 submitted early applications with URM >= 68

    "Additional" means the 1 full-need school that satisfies the first 3.50-3.74 row is not counted again. A second full-need school does count as additional.

    Full need placeholder: every college counts as meeting full need. Keep it a single named column (for example is_full_need, always true) so that replacing it later is a one-line change.

    Near misses

    The tab has 2 jobs: find seniors who could still meet a goal with a small change, and show a counselor why a senior they believed met a goal did not count (for example, the ED college is 3 points under the URM bar).

    Only for the senior's own band, only where the criterion is No, and only from submitted applications.

    A near-bar college is a submitted application of the type the rule needs with URM within 5 points under the bar: 75 to under 80 for the 80% rules, 73 to under 78 for the 78% rule, 63 to under 68 for the 68% rules. For the 3.75+ ED rule only ED applications count; for the 3.75+ EA rule only EA applications count.

    • 1-school rules (3.75+ ED 80%+, 3.50-3.74 full-need 80%+): a near miss when the senior has at least 1 near-bar college. A 3.75+ senior with no ED application at all is a plain No, not a near miss.
    • 2- and 3-school rules (2 EA 78%+, 2 additional 68%+, 3 early 68%+): a near miss when either is true:
    • the senior is exactly 1 qualifying college short, or
    • qualifying colleges plus near-bar colleges reach the required number, so raising the near-bar colleges to the bar would close the gap. A senior with 0 qualifying colleges and 1 near-bar college on the 3-school rule is not a near miss.
    • For the 2 additional 68%+ rule, "qualifying" means qualifying additional colleges, after the 1 full-need college is set aside.
    • Each criterion appears once per senior. "What they have" lists the qualifying and near-bar colleges with their URM rates.

    Tabs

    Summary. Rows: N, the band counts (3.75+, 3.50-3.74, 3.00-3.49, below 3.00), then 2 rows per criterion: "% of N" and "% of band". Columns: Cooper Norcross, Newark Collegiate, Newark Lab, All. Each cell is text like 3% (1/40): the percent rounded to a whole number, then met and the denominator. The denominator is N for "% of N" rows and the band count for "% of band" rows. A band with 0 seniors shows -. The model also carries numeric columns (met, denominator, percent) for each cell, placed to the right in the sheet, for anyone who wants to chart them.

    Roster. 1 row per senior in N. Columns: student, high school, counselor, GPA, GPA band, met all criteria for the band (Yes, No, N/A), count of submitted early applications, then for each criterion a Yes/No/N/A column and a column listing the qualifying colleges (comma-separated, alphabetical, filled only when Yes), then a count of near misses, then every submitted early application with its type and URM rate (for example "Example College (ED) 77%"). The last column answers "why didn't this senior count?" even when a college is more than 5 points under the bar and so never reaches the Near Misses tab.

    Not Submitted Yet. 2 sections, told apart by a section column.

    • A. Seniors in N with no submitted early application. Columns: student, high school, counselor, GPA band, early applications on their list with status Will Apply (college and type, comma-separated), and the earliest due_date__date among them. Seniors with none on their list show a blank list.
    • B. Current seniors at the 3 schools who have at least 1 submitted early application but whose is_ed_ea is not Yes. Columns: student, high school, counselor, GPA band, submitted early applications. The sheet labels this section "Fix the Is ED/EA flag in Overgrad". These seniors are outside N.

    Near Misses. 1 row per senior per missed criterion. Columns: student, high school, counselor, GPA band, criterion, what they have (for example "Example College (ED) at 78.2%"), what would close it (for example "An ED college at 80%+, 1.8 points short", or "1 more EA college at 78%+").

    Refresh

    Connected Sheets refreshes every tab in the source sheet daily, after the Overgrad and dbt runs complete. The Summary tab shows a "data as of" date taken from the data, not the refresh clock.

    Left to the builder

    • Model names, the domain folder for the per-student int_, and how the logic splits between models, within rules A1 to A11.
    • Whether the Summary text cells are built by unpivoting or by explicit union.
    • The source of the "data as of" date (for example the latest Overgrad updated_at in scope).
    • Uniqueness tests at each model's grain and the exposure entry in src/dbt/kipptaf/models/exposures/google-sheets.yml.
    • Step-by-step setup notes for the 2 sheets in the PR, for the person who creates them.

    How to check it

    • N matches a direct count of current seniors at the 3 schools with is_ed_ea = Yes.
    • The 2 seniors from the 2026-10-29 stakeholder report behave as follows. Their names are in the build session's conversation; look them up in the terminal only, and never write them to git, the PR, or the issue. The senior with a submitted ED application at 80%+ and 2 submitted EA applications at 78%+ shows Yes on both 3.75+ criteria. The senior with a submitted ED application at 78.2% and 1 submitted EA application at 78%+ shows No on both 3.75+ criteria and 2 near-miss rows.
    • Summary cells add up: met and denominators per school sum to the All column.
